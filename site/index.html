Ready in three steps

You do these steps one time only.

  1. Install

    Download Webcam Studio. Move it to the Applications folder, then open it.

  2. Allow the camera

    Click Set up meeting camera. System Settings opens. Turn on Webcam Studio under Camera Extensions.

  3. Choose it in your meeting

    In the video settings of your meeting app, choose Webcam Studio Camera.

Questions

Why do I need to allow Webcam Studio in System Settings?

Webcam Studio Camera is a camera extension. macOS asks you one time before a new camera can show in your apps. Webcam Studio opens the correct page in System Settings for you.

Must Webcam Studio stay open during a meeting?

No. Close the window and the app stays in the menu bar. When a meeting app uses Webcam Studio Camera, your camera turns on. When the meeting app stops, your camera turns off.

How do I stop the meeting camera?

Click the Webcam Studio icon in the menu bar and turn off Meeting camera. Meeting apps then show a placeholder picture. To quit the app, click Quit.

Does Webcam Studio upload my video?

No. Webcam Studio does all the work on your Mac. Webcam Studio has no account and sends no video to a server.

Can Webcam Studio open when I log in?

Yes. In Webcam Studio, open Settings and turn on Open at login. This setting is off at the start.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

No. Webcam Studio needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
