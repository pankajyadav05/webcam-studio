gaze-warp.bin holds the pretrained weights of the warping model (flx, 12 point maps, the
"R" eye) from https://github.com/chihfanhsu/gaze_correction, converted to little-endian f32
in the order read by crates/engine/src/gaze.rs. The model is described in:

Chih-Fan Hsu, Yu-Shuen Wang, Chin-Laung Lei and Kuan-Ta Chen. "Look at Me! Correcting Eye
Gaze in Live Video Communication." ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications,
and Applications 15(2), article 38, 2019.
